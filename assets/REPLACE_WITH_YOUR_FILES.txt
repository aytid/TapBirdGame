Replace the files below with your own files, keeping these exact names:
- friend.png       Your friend's face photo (PNG/JPG/WEBP; rename it to friend.png)
- obstacle-1.png   First custom obstacle image
- obstacle-2.png   Second custom obstacle image (optional)
- gameplay.mp3    Audio that loops while playing
- gameover.mp3    Audio that plays after a collision

Keep index.html and the assets folder together. Open index.html in a modern browser.
If an asset is missing, the game uses its built-in fallback artwork/silent audio.
For best results, use a square face image and transparent PNG obstacle images.
